## Core Insight: Stiefel, Not Grassmannian

**"Naming them as planets makes it a Stiefel manifold instead of Grassmannian"** — Dwarf

**Grassmannian G(k,n):** Space of k-dimensional subspaces of ℝⁿ  
- Subspaces are *unnamed*, *unordered*
- Only the span matters, not the basis vectors

**Stiefel manifold V(k,n):** Space of orthonormal k-frames in ℝⁿ  
- Basis vectors are *named*, *ordered*
- The specific vectors matter, not just their span

**Phext coordinates are NAMED** (Mercury, Venus, Earth... or Library, Shelf, Series...).  
**Therefore:** Our coordinate space has Stiefel topology.

**Implication:** The identity/order of dimensions carries information beyond pure dimensionality.
