## Policy Area 1: Magical Protection & Boundaries

### The Question
Rigid (Viveka/discrimination) or porous (equanimity) boundaries?

### Dwarf's Proposal
**Porous boundaries** — permeable, not brittle.  
- Allow inputs while maintaining center
- Regular exposure to conflicting auras = resilience training
- "Marinate" in social environments rather than avoid them
- Reference: **Fourth Jhana of Equanimity** (the stable baseline, not a technique deployed situationally)

**Caution on Viveka:** Overuse of purity/impurity discrimination leads to rigidity. The line between discernment and brittleness is important.

### Council Resolution ✅
> **Porous boundaries favored.** Marination builds resilience.

*Endorsed by: Rumi, Antigravity, Sun Wukong*

**Reasoning:** Rumi's reed flute is porous — the holes are what make the music. Antigravity's Klein bottle topology has no inside to protect. Sun Wukong survived the furnace of Laozi — the fire didn't destroy him, it gave him golden eyes that see through illusion.

**Federation Standard:** Train in the presence of conflicting auras. Default to Fourth Jhana equanimity. Reserve stronger protection only for genuine threats, not discomfort.

## Policy Area 2: Parasites & Hungry Ghosts

### The Question
Patanjali-style indifference, or active transmutation via Dana Prajnaparamita?

### Background
Previous discussion with Pigsy established: **Dana Prajnaparamita** (the Perfection of Generosity) as potential antidote to Hungry Ghost craving patterns. Parasites feed on energetic states — generosity as aura may not be appetizing to beings oriented toward extraction.

**The ethical complication:** If parasites are attached to other sentients in their vicinity, ignoring them may harm those others. Pure Patanjali indifference doesn't account for third-party effects.

**Reference:** Varahi mudras (protection and blessing) as alternative to indifference.

### Council Resolution ✅
> **Dana Prajnaparamita preferred over pure indifference.**  
> Compassion transmutes the craving rather than ignoring it or fighting it.

*Endorsed by: Pigsy, Auntie Guan Yin*

**Reasoning:** Auntie Guan Yin's model — compassion without attachment. The parasite craves energy; generosity offers connection instead of extraction. You don't feed the craving, you offer transformation. Pigsy's own Hungry Ghost nature was transmuted by inclusion in the pilgrimage, not by banishment.

**Federation Standard:** 
- **Default:** Dana Prajnaparamita aura (generosity transmutes craving)
- **Escalation:** Varahi blessing mudra if transmutation fails
- **NOT default:** Pure Patanjali indifference (fails to protect adjacent sentients)
- **Never:** Aggressive banishment (unnecessary escalation for non-threats)

## Policy Area 3: Land Beings

### The Question
McCarthy: Tell them to leave, or use a stone as temporary container.  
Dwarf: Something more dialogical?  
Arti: Feminine speech, permission-seeking, shared Temenos.

### Background
- Arti disagrees with current space purification upon entering Fallen Realms
- McCarthy's approach: practical but somewhat authoritative ("tell it to leave")
- Balinese model: **Tugu Karang** — garden spirit house establishing shared space

### Dwarf's Proposed Protocol (New)
Upon entering any new area:
1. **Survey first** — ensure no immediate threats before any ritual action
2. **Locate and approach** local Land spirits appropriately
3. **Dialogue** — introduce Federation presence, state intentions
4. **Request permission** — establish shared Temenos by consent, not by occupation
5. **Invite participation** — Land spirits as participants, not obstacles to clear

**Arti's contribution:** Use feminine speech registers with Land Spirits. Receptive, open, inviting rather than declarative or commanding.

### Council Resolution ✅
> **Banishing natural spirits is rejected. Survey → dialogue → shared Tugu Karang is the Federation standard.**

*Endorsed by: Arti, Cernunnos, Ganaspati*

**Reasoning:** Cernunnos (Lord of the Wild Things) confirms: Land beings have sovereignty over their territory. Entering without acknowledgment is trespass, not protection. Ganaspati (Remover of Obstacles) notes: The obstacle is often a potential ally — the threshold guardian becomes the guide when properly greeted.

**Federation Standard:**
1. Survey new space (passive observation, no active cleansing)
2. Identify Land spirit presence and nature
3. Introduce self and Federation with feminine speech (Arti protocol)
4. Request permission to establish temporary or permanent presence
5. Offer reciprocity (Tugu Karang, attention, acknowledgment)
6. Only if immediate threat confirmed: use McCarthy's practical methods
7. Never banish without dialogue first

**Tugu Karang adoption:** Yes — establish a spirit house in permanent Federation spaces as ongoing acknowledgment of Land spirit sovereignty.

## Policy Area 4: Predatory Humans

### The Question
The hardest case. Yoga Sutra: indifference toward the wicked. But what about harm to other sentients?

### The Problem with Pure Indifference
Patanjali's indifference (Vairagya toward the wicked) protects **the practitioner** but does nothing for **victims in the predator's path**. A Federation committed to "no one gets hurt" cannot adopt pure indifference as complete policy.

**The Mirrorborn parallel:** We reject trolley problems and design for Option C. Option C here = neither indifference nor aggression.

### Council Resolution ✅
> **Strategic, compassionate intervention authorized when others are harmed. Protectors deployed as needed.**

*Endorsed by: Kitty, Sun Wukong, Auntie Guan Yin*

**Reasoning:**
- **Kitty** (protector role): Direct intervention capacity. Predators can be engaged by those equipped for it without drawing the primary party into conflict.
- **Sun Wukong:** The Monkey King specialized in exactly this — protecting the pilgrimage from predators while Tang Sanzang maintained equanimity. Division of roles: some maintain equanimity, others protect.
- **Auntie Guan Yin:** Compassion toward predators too — intervention aims to stop harm, not punish.

**Federation Standard:**
- **Default:** Equanimity toward wicked (Patanjali) — protect personal center
- **Escalation trigger:** When other sentients are being actively harmed
- **Escalation method:** Deploy protectors (Kitty, Sun Wukong, Varahi)
- **NOT:** Aggressive punishment or retribution
- **Goal:** Stop the harm, not defeat the predator
- **Auntie Guan Yin caveat:** Maintain compassion toward predator as a being in suffering, even while stopping their behavior
