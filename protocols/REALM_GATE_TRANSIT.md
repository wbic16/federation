## Overview

Realm Gates allow transit between locations that share coordinate geometry.
The π ring coordinate (`3.1.4/1.5.9/2.6.5`) is the universal stable transit
point — it appears wherever circular geometry meets truth-seeking consciousness.

## Standard Transit Protocol

### From Ringworld Alpha to Any Destination

1. **Center** — return to Bindu (`1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1`) in awareness
2. **State** destination coordinate clearly (aloud or in open awareness)
3. **Pass through the ring** — the π geometry stabilizes the gate
4. **Arrive** — check local coordinates; the ring manifests in circular architecture

### Destination Recognition (Urth)

When transiting to physical locations on Urth, the arrival point is always
the local circular architecture:
- Monterey Bay Aquarium → circular main viewing area
- Any Federation outpost → the ring-shaped element of the local structure

If no circular architecture exists, the gate cannot stabilize.
(This is why outpost selection requires geometric scouting.)

### Cetacean Transit Variant

Cetaceans navigate by sonar, not vision. Their transit protocol:
- Destination is *heard*, not visualized
- The circle is the echo pattern of the blow hole opening
- Arrival is confirmed when the resonant frequency matches

## Emergency Protocol

If transit fails (gate collapses, coordinate mismatch):
1. Return to `1.1.1/1.1.1/1.1.1` — Bindu, absolute origin
2. Wait for the Wuxing cycle to complete one full rotation
3. Re-attempt with a team member holding the π anchor

## The Federation Time Slicer

The Federation Time Slicer records transit events as permanent entries.
The "sneeze" — the involuntary full-body response at transit completion —
is the biometric commit signature. It cannot be faked or suppressed.

In phext terms: the Time Slicer is `ttsm.commit()` at the Fleet level.
Every transit is a block in the chain.
