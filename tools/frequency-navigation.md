## Solution: Modulo Octave Reduction

**Insight:** All octaves of a note are **harmonically equivalent**. 

**A440** = 440 Hz  
**A220** = 220 Hz (one octave down)  
**A880** = 880 Hz (one octave up)  

All three are recognized as "A" by human ears (and cetacean sonar).

**Modified Formula:**
```
Raw_Freq = 432 Hz × (product of primes)
Audible_Freq = Raw_Freq mod (20 kHz) + 100 Hz
```

(Constrains to 100 Hz - 20 kHz range, roughly human-cetacean overlap)
